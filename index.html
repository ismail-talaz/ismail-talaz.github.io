Ismail Talaz

MSc Computer Science, ETH Zürich

I am a master’s student in Computer Science at ETH Zürich. My research interests span multimodal learning and trustworthy AI, with particular interests in representation learning, interpretability, and the evaluation of foundation models.

I have worked on medical vision-language model safety with Prof. Dr. Paul Liang (MIT), Dr. Jiaee Cheong (Harvard), and Prof. Dr. Sinan Kalkan (METU). As a research intern in the Explainable Machine Learning (EML) group at Helmholtz Munich and TUM, led by Prof. Dr. Zeynep Akata, I studied multimodal representations and the vision-centric capabilities of multimodal large language models. At METU, I also worked on interpretable brain graph learning for Alzheimer’s disease with Prof. Dr. Fatoş Yarman Vural and state-space models for time-series forecasting with Prof. Dr. Sinan Kalkan. I hold a bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering from METU, where I graduated with a 4.00/4.00 GPA as a valedictorian.
